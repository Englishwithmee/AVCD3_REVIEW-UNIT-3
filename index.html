1. Grammar review · Comparisons

Comparatives

short adj + -er + than
cheaper than, hotter than

more + long adj + than
more interesting than

y → ier · big → bigger · good → better · bad → worse

Superlatives

the + short adj + -est
the tallest, the busiest

the most + long adj
the most expensive

good → the best · bad → the worst

(Not) as ... as

as + adjective + as
This bus is as fast as that bus.

not as + adjective + as
My room isn't as big as yours.

Use the base form of the adjective.

2. Grammar review · Past tenses

Past simple

(+) S + V2 / -ed
We visited a castle.

(−) didn't + V1
We didn't visit a castle.

(?) Did + S + V1?

yesterday, last week, two days ago

Past continuous

was / were + V-ing
She was reading at 8 p.m.

(−) wasn't / weren't + V-ing
(?) Was / Were + S + V-ing?

at that moment, at 7 yesterday, while

When / While

Long action: past continuous
Short interruption: past simple

I was walking when it started to rain.

While I was reading, she was cooking.

when often introduces a short event; while often introduces an ongoing action.

✍️ Practice 15 questions each for Past simple, Past continuous, and both tenses together.
